Bio

I was born in Germany, and grew up all over Europe before moving to the United States to attend the University of Chicago. There, I learned to use mathematics to interpret the world around me, and that building software is the most powerful medium for creative self-expression.

I now live in San Francisco, where I learned that the most important ingredients for progress are grit, agency, and optimism. I love this city, not just for its good sunny weather, but for the culture that has shaped my adult life: the beliefs in freedom and technology to create a better tomorrow.

Founding

My professional life is almost entirely constituted by creative partnerships with my friends. I am fortunate to have helped start these companies.

In April 2020, I co-founded Limit with Alex Becker, Yoav Shaked, and Shea McNamara. We changed the daily lives of thousands of insurance professionals with our two products: Limit AI, a suite of AI workflow tools, and Limit Wholesale, our digital insurance brokerage. I served as CEO for five years, from day one until passing the torch when we were acquired by McGowan, one of the oldest, largest, and most respected insurance organizations in the United States.

In August 2025, Aashay Sanghvi, Patrick Mandia, and I began scoping out a new company to improve personal health by focusing on environmental factors. We then co-founded Rose with Eli Bronner and Henry Ellis, and have been supporting them on their journey ever since.

In October 2025, I co-founded Green with Bijan Tehrani, Edward Craven, and Yoav Shaked. We are building a global financial institution for the people and agents of the future.

Coins & trades
Start with 25 coins. Buy startup equity at the Y Combinator house south of the airport for 10 coins; the Secondary purchaser beside Venture exchange pays 20. The grain merchant beside Golden Gate Park sells duck grain for one coin.
Fishing
Click the fishing spot at Fisherman’s Wharf to catch a live fish. Carry one at a time in your water bucket, then talk to the chef at Cotogna to sell it for 5 coins. You can return for another catch afterward.
Airship rope
Hover over clear ground and use Rope to drop a line, climb down, or climb back up. A traveler can pay five coins to climb aboard. Retract the rope before flying.
Tax headquarters
Follow Second Street south. The officials accept all your coins in taxes, or you can question them about the empty high-speed rail pit behind their office.
Tahoe
Follow the highway across the Bay Bridge and northeast to Tahoe, or talk to a SpaceX attendant to board a reusable rocket for one coin each way. Follow the pier from the jet terminal onto the spaceport in the bay. The airship also flies to Tahoe. Ride the gondola up, talk to the summit attendant to rent skis for one coin, then use Ski down. Click to steer and press Space in the air to flip.
Zoo
Visit the zoo west of Lake Merced. Open the tiger’s gate, then keep moving! Animal control will try to return it to its cage. If it catches you, you respawn at UCSF Hospital in the southeast.
Beer
Shanghai Kelly’s in North Beach sells beer for one coin. Each adds 10% inebriation, up to 100%; one percentage point wears off every three seconds. Your character sways as you get tipsier.
Conversations
Use the arrow keys to select a response and Enter to continue. Tax officials return to their choices after each reply; choose Leave or press Escape to finish.
B / bicycle

M / Motorcycle
Talk to the rental clerk beside the zoo’s monkey habitat to rent the conveniently parked motorcycle for five coins before taking the monkey. Click to ride; M parks or finds your rental. The tiger-speed zookeeper pursues monkey thieves and takes the monkey back if he catches you. Trying to steal the motorcycle summons the police.
A / Airship
Rent an airship at the private jet terminal south of the city. Click the basket floor to walk around, or click the ground or minimap to fly. A returns you to the terminal.
V / Forward roll
Tuck into a forward roll, moving 18% faster than walking. Roll along your walking route, or roll in the direction you are facing when standing still. Walls and water still block your path.
D / Dance
Dance until you press D again or start walking. Try your moves at Nightlife in the South Mission.
C / Character
Choose your gender and colors. Men have short hair; women have long hair. Changes apply immediately and are saved in this browser.
R / Ferrari
Borrow a Ferrari at the dealership beyond the Bay Bridge. Click the road or minimap to drive; R parks. Your tank lasts 56% of the drive to Tahoe. The two highway gas stations take all your coins; the dealership and Tahoe gondola refill for free.
SF Fitness / Strength
Visit SF Fitness east of the zoo for bench presses, dumbbell curls, barbell squats, or overhead presses. Use the gym’s exercise options to switch while training; all earn the same strength XP. Level 10 takes about twenty minutes, with harder levels as you improve. Sand Hill Road Capital Partners offers a one-time 1,000-coin investment at level 10. Progress is saved in this browser.
U / Pacifica surfing
Borrow a surfboard at Pacifica beach. Click the water to paddle, then press Space near a foam line to catch a wave. Click to steer while riding; 1 / 2 / 3 perform an Air 360, kickflip, or backflip. Land tricks to earn points. U or F returns you to the beach.
Twitter / sales conference
Three blocks south of SF Zoo: Twitter HQ’s bottle-post backend drains into the ocean. Being inside adds 3% inebriation per second. Next door, buy sales software for 10 coins and pitch it for 20. Buyers think for ten seconds; you can keep exploring. Success rises from 10% sober to 50% at full inebriation. A refusal leaves your copy intact.
Tim Apple / robotaxis
South of Coastal Gardens Road, Tim Apple’s donut campus sells iPhones for 100 coins. Equip yours in Character: all income rises 25%, including fractional bonuses on small payments, but you lose 5 HP every two equipped minutes. Unequipping pauses doomscrolling; death or arrest loses the phone. The nearby Tesla dealer sells one robotaxi business for 100 coins. It pays 20 coins each minute (25 with an equipped iPhone) while the world is open and visible. Partial timers are saved; there are no offline payouts.
Haberdasher
The hat shop just north of the GPU Factory sells nine hats, from a 1-coin folded paper hat to the 100,000-coin Crown of Excess. Talk to the haberdasher to buy and wear one. Purchases are permanent; open Character (C) to switch between owned hats or remove your hat.
Salt conservation
Visit the salt flats southeast of the southern consciousness research institutes. The conservationist offers repeatable courier work: carry one sealed letter to the City Hall clerk for 50 coins, then return for another. Your inventory and Quest Menu track the letter.
Sand / GPU Factory
Collect sacks of sand at the Ocean Beach sand pile. Take them to the GPU Factory beside the southern research institutes and talk to its buyer. Each sack sells for 20 coins. You can carry multiple sacks; your inventory is saved in this browser.
G / Railgun
Ask the scientist at Jurassic Biosciences, in the green belt at the city’s southern seam, for a quest. G equips or stows your railgun. Click a raptor, cow, or kraken to fire from up to five tiles away; click the ground to stop. Green spectres take zero railgun damage—stow it and use your bayonet. Firing at pedestrians brings the police. Cow sandwiches store in your food bag. Captain Ahab waits by the Pequod on the western coast; bring your railgun and food for his kraken hunt. Collect its deck loot for 100 coins and a cape you can wear in Character settings.
F / Windsurf
Borrow a board at Crissy Field; sail anywhere on open water, click a humpback whale to jump over it, and press F to return to shore
